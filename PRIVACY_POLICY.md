# Privacy Policy for 17 Spinteen

**Last Updated:** May 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use **17 Spinteen** ("the App"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information Collection
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).** 
The App operates locally on your device and does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as name, email, or phone number).

## 2. Local Storage & App Data
To improve your user experience, the App stores certain non-personal settings locally on your device:
*   **Preferences:** Your selected K-Pop group and member preferences.
*   **Cached Data:** Metadata and thumbnails of YouTube videos (such as GOING SEVENTEEN episodes) to reduce network usage and loading times.

This data is stored purely locally via your device's native storage mechanisms (e.g., `SharedPreferences`) and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's cache in your device settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services
While the App does not collect personal data, it utilizes third-party services to function properly. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

*   **YouTube Data API:** The App fetches video titles, thumbnails, and URLs from YouTube. By using this feature, you are also bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
*   **Video Redirection:** When you click to watch a video, the App redirects you to the official YouTube platform (either the website or the native App). We do not track your viewing history.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

# 隱私權政策 (繁體中文)

**最後更新日期：** 2026 年 5 月 11 日

感謝您使用 **17 Spinteen**（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）。本隱私權政策旨在協助您了解我們如何處理您的資訊。

使用本應用程式即表示您同意本隱私權政策的條款。

## 1. 資訊收集
**我們不會收集、儲存或傳輸任何個人識別資訊 (PII)。**
本應用程式完全在您的裝置本地運行，不需要您建立帳戶、登入或提供任何個人資料（如姓名、電子郵件或電話號碼）。

## 2. 本地儲存與應用程式資料
為了提升您的使用體驗，本應用程式會在您的裝置上進行本地儲存：
*   **偏好設定：** 您選擇的 K-Pop 團體與成員設定。
*   **快取資料：** YouTube 影片（如 GOING SEVENTEEN 集數）的標題與縮圖，以減少網路流量消耗並提升載入速度。

這些資料僅透過您裝置的原生儲存機制（如 `SharedPreferences`）保存在本地，**絕不會**上傳至我們的伺服器或與任何第三方分享。您可以隨時透過解除安裝本應用程式或清除應用程式快取來刪除這些資料。

## 3. 第三方服務
雖然我們不收集個人資料，但本應用程式使用了第三方服務以提供核心功能。這些服務可能會根據其自身的隱私權政策收集資訊：

*   **YouTube Data API：** 本應用程式會從 YouTube 獲取影片標題、縮圖與網址。使用此功能時，您同時受到 [YouTube 服務條款](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) 與 [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy) 的約束。
*   **影片跳轉：** 當您點擊觀看影片時，本應用程式會將您導向至官方的 YouTube 平台（網站或原生 App）。我們不會追蹤您的觀看紀錄。

## 4. 兒童隱私
本應用程式不會有意收集 13 歲以下兒童的任何個人資訊。

## 5. 隱私權政策的變更
我們可能會不時更新此隱私權政策。建議您定期查看此頁面以獲取任何變更。
